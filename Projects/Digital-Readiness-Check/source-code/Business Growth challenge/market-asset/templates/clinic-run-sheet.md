# Readiness Clinic run sheet (45 minutes)

**Frequency:** monthly · **Format:** online (in-person occasionally in Johannesburg) · **Team:** 1 senior consultant (host), 2-3 interns (prepare and support), 1 note-taker

## Before the session (1 week)

- [ ] Publish the event page with a registration form that asks only for name, email and consent to be contacted about the clinic.
- [ ] Ask 3-5 registrants if they will volunteer their results for live review (written consent, anonymised on screen).
- [ ] Interns prepare a one-page review for each volunteer using the [report template](./sample-results-report.md); the senior consultant checks it.
- [ ] Test audio, screen sharing and the Check itself.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Item | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Welcome, how the Check works, privacy ground rules | Host |
| 5-15 | Latest benchmark findings and the three most common gaps | Host |
| 15-35 | Two or three live reviews: score, quick wins, free DIY steps | Interns, with host support |
| 35-42 | Open questions | All |
| 42-45 | Next steps: book a free 30-minute review, join Coxygen learning paths | Host |

## Ground rules

- Show no organisation's name or data without consent.
- Give DIY steps first; mention paid services only after.
- Do not give legal advice. Suggest a legal adviser for POPIA questions.

## After the session (within 48 hours)

- [ ] Send the recording and slides to attendees who consented.
- [ ] Email each volunteer their written review.
- [ ] Log: attendees, reviews booked, leads, intern hours and one lesson learned.
- [ ] Ask each intern for a short reflection and add the work to their portfolio.

## Success measures for each clinic

Registrations · attendance rate · reviews booked within 14 days · interns involved · attendee satisfaction (1-5).
