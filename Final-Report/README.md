## Reflection
During my WIL placement at Tobb Technologies, I learned how the concepts from my studies work in real projects. I installed Ubuntu, managed files from the terminal, and practised Haskell, the language used on the Cardano blockchain. I also tested the Coxy wallet application and documented its issues for the development team. I documented the Coxy Wallet AI Music Contest’s purpose, user journey, features, technologies, accessibility and test cases. I improved the Coxy AI Donations App by fixing security flaws such as cross-site scripting and adding features like search, filtering and CSV export. Presenting to the CTO taught me to explain technical ideas in terms of business value, and working with my team taught me the value of clear communication, regular testing and consistent standards.

Some tasks were challenging. Combining our work in the group project meant fixing problems that appeared when different parts of the code came together, and I had to troubleshoot the document upload. The biggest challenge was hosting the Zonke site on the web. Running a site locally is very different from deploying it, and I had to work out how cPanel works and troubleshoot until all the pages loaded correctly. Solving these problems improved my problem-solving skills and gave me practical experience of the full process, from building and testing to deploying a working application.
